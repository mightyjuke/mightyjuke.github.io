Playing Towerwick

How do I move and build?

Drag on the battlefield to move your hero. Attacks happen automatically. Walk near a marked building pad, release your movement touch, then press Build. Collect dropped gold and return to your defenses to upgrade them.

How does the campaign work?

The campaign has 200 levels across twenty regions. Finish a level to unlock the next. Boss encounters appear every tenth level. Earlier levels remain available from the campaign map.

What happens when I close the game?

Completed levels, medals and settings are saved on your device. An interrupted battle resumes from a saved wave opening, so part of a wave may be replayed. If an update changes a level, the battle may restart while completed levels and medals remain.

Can I transfer or restore my progress?

Towerwick 1.0 has no developer account or cloud-save service. Progress belongs to the local app installation. Before deleting the app or changing devices, consider your device backup options. Support cannot recover progress from a game server.

How can I reduce battery use?

Open Settings and turn on Battery saver to cap the game at 30 frames per second. You can also turn off screen shake and vibration, or adjust music and effects independently.

Does the game need internet or contain purchases?

Campaign play works offline. The ad-supported update offers optional rewarded wave continues and occasional ads between completed battles; ads require an internet connection and may be unavailable. There are no production in-app purchases. Opening this website or sending a support email also uses your internet connection.

How do I reset my campaign?

Open Settings, choose Reset campaign progress and confirm the reset. This erases completed levels, medals and records from the local game. There is no undo, so only confirm when you want to start again.
