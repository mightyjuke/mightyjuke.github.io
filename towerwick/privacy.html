Privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026 · Applies to the ad-supported update

Your campaign stays on your device. Towerwick 1.0 plays offline and does not require an account.

Towerwick: Tower Defense is developed by Kevin Mok. This policy covers the ad-supported Towerwick update, this support website, and information you choose to send by email. Earlier ad-free builds do not initialize the advertising SDK described below.

Game dataThis websiteContact

Data in the game

The game stores campaign progress, medals, records, settings and battle checkpoints in its local app storage. It does not send these gameplay records to a developer-operated server or to advertising partners.

The ad-supported update offers optional rewarded wave continues and occasional ads between completed battles through Google AdMob. It has no account system or production in-app purchases. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or device location services.

Advertising and privacy choices

Google AdMob may process your IP address (including approximate location derived from it), device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and crash and performance diagnostics to provide, measure and protect advertising. Google may share data with advertising partners according to your choices and its policies. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s SDK data disclosure.

Google’s User Messaging Platform asks for consent or provides opt-out choices where required. You can revisit these through Ad privacy choices in the game’s Settings when the option is available. Withdrawing consent updates future ad requests and clears loaded ads.

On iOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission controls access to the advertising identifier (IDFA). You can decline tracking and keep playing. Change permission under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Towerwick does not retrieve, log or store your IDFA; Google’s SDK can access it only when the operating system permits. We disable Google’s publisher first-party ID feature.

Campaign play remains available without internet access. Ads need a connection and may be unavailable depending on consent, network access or inventory. No reward is offered for granting tracking permission.

Removal and device backups

You can erase campaign progress using Reset campaign & settlement in Settings. Deleting the app and its data removes its local records. Progress reset preserves your settings.

Your device may include app data in backups according to your Apple account and device settings. Removing local data does not necessarily remove existing backups. Towerwick does not provide its own cloud-save service; manage backups through your device’s settings.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store downloads, any app-purchase transactions, and optional device diagnostics under its own policies and your settings. See Apple’s Privacy Policy. These platform services are separate from Towerwick’s local gameplay records.

This website

This site does not use advertising cookies, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or a sign-up form. Its font and styles are served with the site.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub’s Privacy Statement. External websites have their own privacy practices.

Support and privacy requests

If you email support, Kevin receives your email address and the information you choose to send so he can respond. Do not send passwords or payment-card information. Support emails may be retained to resolve your request and keep a record of the correspondence.

For questions or privacy requests, including requests from parents or guardians, contact Kevin Mok at kevinmokex@gmail.com. You can request deletion of support correspondence; some records may need to be retained where required by law.

Policy changes

If the game’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date. The version stated above identifies the game release covered by this policy.
