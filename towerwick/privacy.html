Privacy policy

Towerwick 1.0 · Effective November 7, 2026

Your campaign stays on your device. Towerwick 1.0 plays offline and does not require an account.

Towerwick: Tower Defense is developed by Kevin Mok. This policy covers version 1.0 of the game, this support website, and information you choose to send by email.

Game dataThis websiteContact

Data in the game

The game stores campaign progress, medals, records, settings and battle checkpoints in its local app storage. It does not send these records to a developer-operated server or collect, sell or share personal information through the game.

Version 1.0 has no advertising, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Removal and device backups

You can erase campaign progress using Reset campaign progress in Settings. Deleting the app and its data removes its local records. Progress reset preserves your settings.

Your device may include app data in backups according to your Apple account and device settings. Removing local data does not necessarily remove existing backups. Towerwick does not provide its own cloud-save service; manage backups through your device’s settings.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store downloads, any app-purchase transactions, and optional device diagnostics under its own policies and your settings. See Apple’s Privacy Policy. These platform services are separate from Towerwick’s local gameplay records.

This website

This site does not use advertising cookies, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or a sign-up form. Its font and styles are served with the site.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub’s Privacy Statement. External websites have their own privacy practices.

Support and privacy requests

If you email support, Kevin receives your email address and the information you choose to send so he can respond. Do not send passwords or payment-card information. Support emails may be retained to resolve your request and keep a record of the correspondence.

For questions or privacy requests, including requests from parents or guardians, contact Kevin Mok at kevinmokex@gmail.com. You can request deletion of support correspondence; some records may need to be retained where required by law.

Policy changes

If the game’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date. The version stated above identifies the game release covered by this policy.
