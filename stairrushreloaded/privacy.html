Privacy policy

Stairs Rush Reloaded · Updated October 25, 2026

Stairs Rush Reloaded is developed by Kevin Mok (JukeDev Co.). This policy covers the game and this website. Privacy practices depend on your platform, app version, the features you use, and your privacy choices. The platform details below describe version 1.3.5 (build 47); earlier versions may differ as explained below.

Your game progress is saved on your device. The Android build 47 is offline. On iPhone and iPad, advertising and Apple services can process information as described below. You do not need a separate developer account to play.

Game dataAdvertisingYour choicesContact

Game data on your device

The game stores settings, personal bests, gems, unlocked and selected pilots, purchase entitlements, transaction identifiers, and reward records in its app storage. Local backup files help recover a save. A score waiting for submission to Game Center may also be stored locally.

The remaster does not upload its save file to a developer-operated game server. Core gameplay works without an internet connection.

Android

Android version 1.3.5 (build 47) has no internet permission and does not use advertising, online leaderboards, or in-app billing. Automatic Android cloud backup is disabled for this build.

This description is specific to build 47. Older Android releases included third-party advertising and analytics software, so the offline-build description should not be read as a statement that every previous version collected no data. If you have questions about an older version, contact us with its version number.

Advertising on iPhone and iPad

Supported iOS versions use Google AdMob for banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements. Google's advertising services may process IP addresses and IP-derived general location, device identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information to deliver, measure, and maintain advertising services.

The information processed depends on consent choices, device permissions, and the features in the installed version. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising data disclosure.

Privacy and advertising choices

Google's User Messaging Platform presents applicable privacy messages. When available, open Settings → Privacy Options in the remaster to revisit your choices. Earlier versions may place privacy options on the Information screen.

Apple's tracking permission controls access to the advertising identifier on iOS. You can change this permission in your device's Settings. You can decline tracking and continue playing. Declining tracking does not prevent all advertising-related processing, such as receiving an IP address to deliver an ad.

Remove Ads disables banners and automatic interstitial advertisements. Optional rewarded ads may remain available if you choose to watch them. Removing local game progress does not reset advertising consent or Apple's tracking permission.

Apple Game Center

On supported iOS versions, Game Center handles authentication, leaderboards, and eligible score submissions. Eligible scores may submit automatically while you are signed in; the game can retry a pending score later. Build 47's Abilities records stay on the device, while eligible saved Classic Standard scores can still submit.

Your Game Center profile and scores may be visible according to Apple's settings and policies. Manage Game Center through your device settings and see Game Center & Privacy.

In-app purchases

Apple processes purchases on supported iOS versions. The game receives product, purchase-status, transaction, and entitlement information to verify purchases, deliver purchased items, avoid duplicate rewards, and restore eligible ownership. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details. See App Store & Privacy.

Retention and removal

Local game records remain until updated through play or removed using your device's app-data controls. Deleting the app's data removes local progress. This does not delete information held separately by Apple or Google, remove Game Center records, or cancel purchases.

Apple and Google retain information according to their own policies and provide separate account and privacy controls. Contact us if you need help identifying which service handles a particular request.

This website

This website does not use analytics scripts, advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or a sign-up form. Fonts and images are served with the site. GitHub Pages hosts it and logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

When you follow an external link, such as the App Store, that service's own privacy policy applies.

Support and privacy requests

If you email support, Kevin receives your email address and the information you choose to send to answer your request. Avoid sending passwords, payment-card information, or other sensitive details.

For privacy questions or requests, including requests from parents or guardians, contact Kevin Mok at kevinmokex@gmail.com. Include your platform and app version where relevant so we can identify the applicable features and data handling.

Changes to this policy

Changes will be published on this page with an updated date. Check this page when installing an update, since available services can differ between versions and platforms.
