Privacy policy

Effective 2 October 2026 · Squadstorm: Zombie Shooter

Squadstorm: Zombie Shooter is developed by Kevin Mok. This policy applies to builds with AdMob ads.

Squadstorm saves game progress on your device and shows ads through Google AdMob.

Game data

Progress, settings, coins, and records stay in the app's private storage. Android Auto Backup may include this data in your Google backup. Settings → Reset all progress erases game progress; uninstalling removes the local save. No game account is required.

Ads

An interstitial may appear between levels after every five successful campaign runs. Optional rewarded ads can revive a campaign run or boost earned coins. The core game works offline. Ads and privacy messages require an internet connection.

Advertising data

Google's advertising SDK may process IP-derived general location, device identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic/performance information for advertising and measurement. Data handling depends on your consent choices and device permissions. See Google's Privacy Policy and advertising technology information.

Consent and tracking

Google's User Messaging Platform presents applicable privacy messages. Where required, Settings → Ad Privacy Choices lets you revisit consent. On iOS, an IDFA explainer precedes Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt when eligible. You may decline tracking and keep playing. iOS controls access to IDFA; you can change permission in system Settings. Resetting game progress does not reset advertising consent or the system tracking permission.

Sharing

Daily Challenge cards are saved locally when you tap Save Card. You choose whether and where to share them.

Audience

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Kevin Mok at kevinmokex@gmail.com.
